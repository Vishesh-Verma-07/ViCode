# Simple Skill: Making a Cup of Tea

## Overview
This skill demonstrates how to make a basic cup of tea using a kettle and teabag.

## Steps
1. Fill the kettle with fresh water and turn it on.
2. While the water heats, place a teabag in your favorite mug.
3. Once the water has reached a rolling boil, pour it over the teabag.
4. Let the tea steep for 2–3 minutes, then remove the teabag.
5. Add milk or sugar to taste, stir, and enjoy!

## Tips
- Use filtered water for better flavor.
- Experiment with steeping time to find your preferred strength.
- For iced tea, let the hot tea cool then refrigerate over ice.
