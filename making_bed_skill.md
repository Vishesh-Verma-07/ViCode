# Simple Skill: Making a Bed

## Overview
A quick guide to making your bed neatly in the morning.

## What You'll Need
- Fitted sheet
- Flat sheet
- Blanket or duvet
- Pillow(s)
- Optional: bedspread or decorative throw

## Steps
1. **Remove any clutter.** Clear pillows, blankets, and other items from the bed.
2. **Spread the fitted sheet.** If not already on the mattress, put on the fitted sheet, smoothing out wrinkles.
3. **Add the flat sheet.** Lay the flat sheet over the fitted sheet, centering it with the top edge about 6–10 inches below the head of the mattress.
4. **Fold the top edge.** Fold the top hem of the flat sheet down over the mattress (or over the blanket/duvet) to create a clean edge.
5. **Place the blanket or duvet.** Lay your blanket or duvet evenly over the sheet.
6. **Fluff and arrange pillows.** Place pillows at the head of the bed, fluffing them for a tidy look.
7. **Optional decorative touch.** Add a throw or bedspread at the foot of the bed for style.

## Tips
- **Smooth as you go.** Run your hand over each layer to remove bumps before moving on.
- **Consistent folding.** Folding the same amount each time creates a uniform look.
- **Make it a habit.** Doing this daily keeps the bedroom looking fresh.
