# Simple Skill: Folding a Fitted Sheet

## Overview
Folding a fitted sheet can be tricky, but with a little practice you can master it in a few easy steps.

## What You'll Need
- A clean fitted sheet
- A flat surface (table or bed)

## Steps
1. **Turn the sheet inside out.** Hold the sheet by two adjacent corners (e.g., the top-left and top-right) with the inside facing you.
2. **Put your hands in the corners.** Insert your hands into the two corners you’re holding, keeping them inside out.
3. **Fold the first corner.** Bring the second corner (right hand) over the first (left hand) and fold it inside out, so now you have both left corners in your left hand and a right corner in your right hand.
4. **Add the third corner.** Reach down and pull the third corner (bottom-left) up with your left hand, folding it over the others, again inside out.
5. **Finish with the fourth corner.** Finally, pull the last corner (bottom-right) up and fold it over the bundle. You should now have all four corners gathered together, with the sheet folded inside out.
6. **Lay the sheet flat.** Place the folded bundle on the flat surface.
7. **Fold into a rectangle.** Fold the sheet in half lengthwise, then in half again (or as many times as needed) to produce a neat rectangular shape.

## Tips
- **Practice makes perfect.** The first few attempts may feel awkward, but muscle memory sets in quickly.
- **Smooth out wrinkles** as you go; a smooth sheet folds neater.
- **Store with your other linens** once folded neatly.
