# Simple Skill: Making a Quick Peanut Butter & Jelly Sandwich

## Overview
A fast, no‑cook lunch that comes together in minutes.

## What You'll Need
- 2 slices of bread
- Peanut butter
- Jelly or jam
- Knife (butter knife or spreader)
- Cutting board (optional)

## Steps
1. **Lay out the bread.** Place the two slices on a clean surface or plate.
2. **Spread the peanut butter.** Using the knife, scoop a dollop of peanut butter and spread it evenly over one slice, from edge to edge.
3. **Spread the jelly.** On the second slice, spread a similar amount of jelly or jam.
4. **Combine.** Place the jelly‑side onto the peanut‑butter‑side, pressing gently so the filling adheres.
5. **Cut (optional).** Slice the sandwich diagonally or into strips for easier eating.
6. **Enjoy!** Pack it for a lunchbox or eat right away.

## Tips
- **Warm the knife** slightly if the peanut butter is very stiff; it spreads smoother.
- **Experiment with flavors.** Try honey, sliced bananas, or cream cheese instead of jelly.
- **Keep it neat.** Wipe any spills from the edges before closing.
